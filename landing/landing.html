Cards

Same card, wherever it came from.

An email reply, a calendar clash, an agent asking for approval: each one is a question with a few answers.

Suggestions come with the facts checked

It looks at everyone’s calendar before it suggests a time.

  • New events are marked as new
  • Updated invites go out only after you choose

Risky actions never decide themselves

Production data, money and anything irreversible can’t be swiped away or time out. You confirm twice.

  • Risk is labeled with an icon and a word
  • The suggestion is always the safe option

Not what you want? Say so.

Tell it “I have a wedding on Saturday” and it checks your calendar and comes back with new options. You still finish with one number.

  • The first options stay, folded
  • Where it can’t send for you, it copies the reply

News you don’t need to act on, in one place

Release dates, deploy results, billing estimates. Worth knowing, nothing to decide.

  • Promote anything to a card in one tap
  • Your corrections improve the sorting

Failures come back as questions

No bare error messages. You get what went wrong, where things stand, and ways to fix it.

  • One broken connection doesn’t stop the others
  • It says clearly that nothing went out

Trust

At first, it does nothing on its own.

You decide what to hand over. Everything it does is logged, and anything reversible can be undone.

It asks about everything

After you give the same answer five times, it asks whether to handle it from now on.

Ten seconds to undo

Replies, invites and comments wait ten seconds before they go out.

Money always asks

Payments, production data and irreversible actions are rules you can’t turn off.

Emails don’t give orders

Your decisions and its actions, side by side

The activity log shows what happened, when, and under which rule. Turn auto-handling on or off from the same place.

FAQ

Questions

Does it read my KakaoTalk chats?

No. KakaoTalk has no official way to read chats, so we don’t. Add the CatchUp KakaoTalk channel to get your cards there, and paste in any conversation you want sorted.

Does it work while my devices are off?

Yes. The assistant runs in your own space in the cloud. Your cards will be waiting when you’re back.

Will it send replies without asking?

Not at first. It only does what you choose, and anything outgoing waits ten seconds so you can undo it. It offers to take over only after the same answer keeps coming up.

Is my data kept apart from other people’s?

Every person gets a separate space in the cloud, with their data and connections encrypted under their own key.

When can I try it?

We’re building it now. The web app comes first, then iPhone and iPad. In English and Korean.
